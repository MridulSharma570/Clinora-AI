# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a respectful, inclusive, harassment-free student open-source project. Participation must never require disclosing health information or personal records.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and respectful across experience levels and identities.
- Focus feedback on code, evidence, accessibility, safety, and user impact.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, copyright, and the project's narrow educational scope.
- Use synthetic examples only; immediately remove and report accidental sensitive material through a private channel.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized or demeaning conduct, sustained disruption, publishing private information, real patient data, credentials, malicious exploits, or pressure to add unsafe diagnostic/treatment functionality.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project spaces and official project representation. [Project Owner] at [Institution] may privately review reports and take proportionate action, including moderation or contribution restrictions, subject to applicable institutional procedures. This is a student-project template, not legal advice; professional review is required before public/commercial use.
