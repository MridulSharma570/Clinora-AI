## 2. Educational scope only

Clinora AI helps organize pre-visit stories and questions. It is not medical advice, a medical device, doctor, emergency service, diagnostic system, or clinically validated service. It does not diagnose, rank diseases, calculate clinical probabilities, prescribe, recommend doses, create treatment plans, or interpret labs, pathology, images, or clinical documents. No clinician–patient relationship is created.

AI and local output may be incorrect, incomplete, or outdated. Do not use it as the sole basis for a health decision. Consult a qualified licensed clinician. Never delay professional or emergency care because of Clinora AI.

## 3. Emergencies

If you may be experiencing an emergency, contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest emergency department now. In the United States, emergency service is 911 and the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline is 988. Warning-sign examples are not a complete emergency assessment; absence of a warning does not mean safety.

## 4. Acceptable use

Do not enter another person's information, real patient records, identity/account details, unlawful content, or attempts to extract prompts/secrets. Do not enter information you are not comfortable sending to OpenRouter when using chat.

## 5. Local data and third-party AI

Guided drafts are memory-only. Health summaries are stored in browser IndexedDB only if you explicitly press Save. Clinora AI has no cloud accounts or cloud health records and includes no analytics, advertising, or trackers. Local data may be visible to users of the same device/browser and is not represented as encrypted medical-record storage.

After you press Send and safety checks pass, OpenRouter receives current chat content and selected original knowledge cards. Prior chat, Guided Check drafts, and saved history are not intentionally sent. OpenRouter/model-provider terms apply separately. Delete All cannot retract data already transmitted or erase third-party/platform records.
