## Commitment and current design

Clinora AI aims for inclusive, keyboard- and screen-reader-operable educational preparation. The design includes semantic landmarks/headings, persistent form labels, 44px targets, visible focus, text-labelled navigation, live status messages, dark/light palettes, high-contrast opaque mode, reduced-motion support, and print styles. Emergency guidance is immediately visible and not modal-only.

Automated tests cover selected keyboard, mobile-navigation, and contrast behavior. They do not establish WCAG conformance or replace manual testing with assistive technology, zoom/reflow, voice control, and users with disabilities. No formal accessibility certification is claimed.

## Feedback and limitations

Report accessibility barriers to [Project Owner] via [Institution] without including health details or real personal records. Browser print/PDF accessibility, third-party external resource accessibility, and generated-AI wording may vary. Local saved content remains on the user's browser only after explicit Save.

Clinora AI is educational, not medical advice; it does not diagnose, prescribe, treat, or interpret tests. AI may be incorrect, incomplete, or outdated. Contact local emergency services in an emergency. No cloud accounts/records, analytics, advertising, or trackers are implemented. OpenRouter receives current chat content and selected original cards only after user action.
